# Alert Categories Guide

Ten configurable categories are seeded per organisation when the add-on is enabled:

1. Welfare concerns  
2. Missed appointments  
3. Repeated missed support visits  
4. Reported incidents  
5. Self-reported concerns  
6. Housing instability  
7. Repeated maintenance concerns affecting safety  
8. Unresolved complaints  
9. Staff-reported safeguarding concerns  
10. Other authorised safeguarding concerns  

System categories (`is_system=true`) should not be deleted; administrators may deactivate or extend descriptions.

Categories do **not** label a tenant as vulnerable automatically.
