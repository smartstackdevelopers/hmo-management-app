# Privacy and consent

- AI disclosure played before conversation (`ai_disclosure_played`).
- Consent recorded per session; decline ends call without retention of content.
- **No call recording** unless `recording_enabled` and `privacy_review_complete` (both off by default).
- No voice biometrics or speaker identity.
- Turn text stored as short summaries only — not full audio.
- DPIA assessment **PENDING APPROVAL** before production telephony.

See `docs/phase7/privacy/` for controller processes.
