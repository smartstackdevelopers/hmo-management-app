# Landlord — training guide

**Who:** `landlord` role (portfolio owner / delegate).  
**Home:** `/dashboard/landlord` — **Landlord enterprise**

## Portfolio

1. Sign in → **Landlord enterprise**.
2. View properties, rooms, rent records, inspections (per permissions).
3. Use filters and tables — avoid exporting lists to personal email.

## Vacancy & tenancy

- Property/room records via **Properties** and **Rooms** where granted.
- Tenancy context links to rent ledger rows when configured.

## Maintenance oversight

- **Maintenance** lists for your properties — read status; contractors update work orders.
- Do not assign yourself contractor tasks unless you also hold contractor role.

## Reports

- Portfolio analytics in landlord dashboard and linked report categories.
- Rent records: enter via permitted UI only — no spreadsheet bypass.

## Permissions you typically lack

- Other landlords’ data, full org audit, tenant clinical notes (unless explicitly granted).

## Mobile

Landlords may use web primarily; mobile staff shell is for support roles.

## FAQs

**Q: I cannot see a tenant name?**  
A: Landlord sees property-level data; tenant PII may be limited by org policy.
